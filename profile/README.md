## 🧠 Overview

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is an open-world role-playing game from CD Projekt Red, set in a vast fantasy world shaped by political conflict, ancient magic, and dangerous creatures. Players take the role of Geralt of Rivia, a professional monster hunter searching for Ciri while navigating a story filled with consequential choices.

Explore bustling settlements, remote wilderness, war-torn regions, and mysterious ruins. Take on contracts, investigate supernatural threats, develop combat abilities, and interact with characters whose stories can change according to your decisions.

## Additional Information for Windows Users

The Witcher 3 offers extensive graphics and performance settings, allowing players to adjust resolution, textures, shadows, and other visual options. Higher settings and advanced rendering features require more capable hardware.

Keep your graphics drivers updated and select settings appropriate for your PC. The game supports keyboard and mouse controls as well as compatible gamepads. Save regularly, especially before difficult encounters or major story decisions.
